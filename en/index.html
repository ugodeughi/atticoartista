I · The view

Nothing in the way,
only the horizon

The eye roams freely across Lake Maggiore to the mountain peaks. No buildings opposite, no obstructions: only water, land and sky. A view that belongs to those who live here.

The lake is never the same: from the gallery and the terrace you witness, day after day, a natural work of art that never repeats itself.

II · The residence

The luxury
of silence

Within the residence, the penthouse enjoys a privileged position: secluded, sheltered, away from prying eyes. All the comfort of an exclusive complex, with the rare tranquillity of a home suspended between the trees and the lake. Here silence is not absence: it is the wind in the trees, the water and the sky.

The estate is managed by S.I.F. Italia S.p.A. and offers a security service with a caretaker on site every weekday.

The swimming pool

A two-level pool set in greenery: a round shallow pool, ideal for children, and a larger main pool for swimming, reached by a flight of steps. Sun loungers, parasols, tables, indoor hot showers and an outdoor one, toilets and separate changing rooms.

The private beach

Reserved exclusively for residents, right on the lakeshore: private access to a stretch of pebble beach, a shaded tree-lined area and a jetty for mooring.

The marina

Pier and moorings reserved exclusively for berth holders, with the option of renting a berth on the floating pontoons. Open views of the lake and the mountains.

Tennis

Two tennis courts in the large wooded park, with benches and seating areas. Sport just a few steps from home.

The park

Cedars, palms, azaleas and hydrangeas tended every week: order and beauty all around the residence buildings.

The garages

Every apartment has its own garage, plus unallocated parking spaces within the park.

The location

Between the San Maurizio hill and the lake

Via Arcipreturale 36, San Maurizio, Ghiffa (VB). The estate stretches from the park down to the shores of Lake Maggiore.

Living area

A spacious, striking living room flooded with natural light through large floor-to-ceiling windows. The fireplace creates a warm, welcoming atmosphere. The semi-open kitchen ensures visual continuity with just the right degree of privacy.

Sleeping area

Three generous bedrooms. The master bedroom opens through large windows onto the panoramic loggia; the second and third bedrooms each have a small loggia overlooking the garden.

Office & tasting room

A versatile, high-end space: a private study for remote working or an elegant tasting room for wines, herbal teas and other delicacies.

Bathrooms & utilities

First bathroom with a shower featuring a sauna function. Second bathroom with bathtub and laundry area. Panoramic loggia equipped with a barbecue.

Finishes and systems

Fine travertine in the main rooms, refined rustic flooring in two bedrooms. Two fixed skylights. Independent heating. Dual aspect ensures natural ventilation even in summer.

Ancillary spaces

Private tiled cellar-laundry room on the mezzanine floor, with water connection, power sockets and shelving. A shaft is already in place for a small new-generation lift, which can be installed subject to approval by the owners' meeting.
